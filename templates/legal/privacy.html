Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Social Media Analytics Tool is a personal, non-commercial portfolio project. This policy explains what it reads from connected accounts, how that information is stored, and how to remove it.

Information the service reads

  • TikTok: your TikTok account ID, display name, and username, plus your public videos' titles or descriptions, publish dates, links, cover images, and view, like, comment, and share counts.
  • Instagram: your Instagram account ID and username, plus your posts' type, publish date, link, image, and like, comment, view, share, and save counts.
  • YouTube: public channel and video information for the channels you import, using YouTube's API Services.
  • Uploads and manual entries: the post information you type in or upload in a CSV file.

The service does not read private messages, comments' contents, contact lists, or anything outside the items listed above.

How information is used

Information is used only to show analytics to the operator of this service: totals, charts, growth over time, and posting-time recommendations. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How information is stored

  • Data is stored in this service's own database.
  • Access tokens from TikTok and Instagram are encrypted before they are stored and are never shown in logs.
  • The service uses a single session cookie for security (protecting forms and logins). It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Removing your information

  • Disconnect on the Accounts page deletes the stored access tokens. For TikTok, it also asks TikTok to revoke the app's access.
  • You can also remove the app's access in TikTok's or Instagram's own settings at any time.
  • To have imported posts and metrics deleted, contact the operator using the link below.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be raised on the project's GitHub page (opens in a new tab).
